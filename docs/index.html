What's included

More than the standard three difficulties.

Use the extra modes when you want them, or start a regular board and play.

01

Daily challenge

Play one fixed no-guess board each day. Daily results have their own history and a spoiler-free share card.

02

Practice and tutorial

Use explained hints, undo moves, or follow the interactive lesson. Practice games do not affect your records.

11⚑ 11
03

Controls built for touch

Tap to reveal and hold to flag. Moving off a cell cancels the move, and large boards can be panned and zoomed.

Local saves and backups

Games, settings, streaks, and best times are stored on your device. You can export or import a backup from Settings.

Game options

Choose a preset or make your own board.

Play Beginner, Intermediate, Expert, or a custom board. You can also enable no-guess presets, question marks, a different hold time, and light, dark, or device colors.

  • ✓ Classic and no-guess play
  • ✓ Custom boards from 5 × 5 to 50 × 50
  • ✓ Local statistics and recent results
  • ✓ Accessible labels and actions
  • ✓ Progress export and import
