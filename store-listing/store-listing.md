## Full description

Minescape is a modern, thoughtful take on the classic mine-clearing puzzle. Choose a board, uncover every safe square, and flag the hidden mines—at home, on a commute, or anywhere else. Everything works offline, with no ads, accounts, analytics, or internet permission.

Play your way

• Pick Beginner, Intermediate, or Expert for familiar classic boards.
• Build a custom board from 5 × 5 to 50 × 50 with your preferred mine density.
• Choose classic generation or no-guess presets designed to be solved through deduction.
• Take on one shared Daily Challenge every day, then review and share your official result.

Learn and improve

• Follow an interactive three-step lesson for revealing, flagging, and chording.
• Turn on Practice mode for explained hints and undo without affecting your records.
• Review detailed statistics, personal-best times, win rate, and streaks.
• Retry the same board to learn from a loss, or start a fresh puzzle at the same difficulty.

Comfortable controls

• Tap to reveal and hold to flag, or switch to a dedicated Flag mode.
• Zoom and pan large boards, then return instantly to Fit board.
• Enable question marks, adjust hold duration, and turn tap-to-chord on or off.
• Use accessible cell labels and actions at any zoom level.

Made for Android

Minescape uses a bright Material 3 design with light, dark, and device themes. On supported devices, Material You colors match your wallpaper. Layouts adapt to phones, tablets, landscape play, and larger text. Optional haptics and system-aware motion settings help the game feel at home on your device.

Private by design

Your games, settings, statistics, and daily history stay on your device. Export a backup whenever you want and import it later through Android's document picker.

Whether you want a quick classic board, a deduction-only challenge, or a relaxed practice session, Minescape gives you a polished puzzle experience without getting in the way.
